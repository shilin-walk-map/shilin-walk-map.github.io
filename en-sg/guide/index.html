How to get to Shilin Night Market

From Taipei Main Station

Take the Red / Tamsui-Xinyi Line from R10 Taipei Main toward Tamsui or Beitou. Get off at R15 Jiantan and use Exit 1.

From Ximending

At G12 Ximen, take the Green / Songshan-Xindian Line toward Songshan to Zhongshan. Change to the Red Line toward Tamsui or Beitou, get off at R15 Jiantan and use Exit 1.

For the first and last MRT trains, bus 218, and return journeys to Taipei Main or Ximending, use the transport section on the interactive map. Official timetable links are included there.

Practical questions

Which MRT station should I use?

For the walking route shown on this map, use Jiantan Station (R15), Exit 1. The name of the night market is Shilin, but this route starts at Jiantan.

Can I print the map?

Yes. Each edition has a one-page A4 landscape PDF. The QR code opens the multilingual online map. Print in landscape at 100% / actual size.

How do I navigate to a food shop?

Open the interactive map, tap its numbered pin, check the Chinese shop name and address, then choose Google Maps or Apple Maps. Walking directions open in that service.

Where are the restrooms?

Restroom icons are marked on the map. Ask staff before using a restroom inside a shop; access can depend on the location.

About this map and its sources

The order is an editorial arrangement for this map, not an official popularity ranking or a visitor survey. Editions may have different food selections and orders. Chinese shop names are kept for matching signs and map listings. Short food descriptions are editorial translations; referenced wording and links are shown when available.

Addresses use the previously confirmed map-destination data. This page does not claim that every shop was independently visited. Opening hours, prices and availability are not inferred. Check the shop and official transport information before travelling.
